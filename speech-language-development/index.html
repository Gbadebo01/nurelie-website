Speech & Language

Support for Your Child's Speech & Language Development

Wondering if your toddler is talking enough, worried about a late talker, or just looking for ways to encourage language at home — Nurélie helps you think it through, without diagnosing anything.

Home/Parenting Challenges/Speech & Language

Speech and language questions bring out a lot of comparison — to milestone charts, to other kids, to a cousin's toddler who talks in full sentences. Nurélie won't diagnose a speech delay, but it can help you understand general patterns and think through when to loop in a specialist.

Tell Harlow what you're noticing, and Nurélie factors in your child's age and context to suggest everyday ways to support communication, and flags when a professional opinion is worth seeking.

What this can look like at home

  • Your toddler is 2 and says far fewer words than you expected.
  • You're not sure if a slight stutter or mispronunciation is typical or worth mentioning to a doctor.
  • You want practical ways to encourage talking during everyday routines, not a formal program.
  • Family members have different opinions about whether you should be "worried."

Why the same behavior can have different reasons

We don't diagnose why — but the same moment often has more than one cause, and that shapes what helps:

  • Speech and language development varies widely between children, even at the same age.
  • Bilingual or multilingual households often show different (not necessarily delayed) patterns.
  • Some children are strong understanders (receptive language) before they're strong talkers (expressive language).
  • Hearing, oral-motor development, and social engagement can all play a role.

What parents often try

  • Comparing word counts against generic milestone charts found online.
  • Waiting to see if it resolves on its own without a clear sense of when to act.
  • Searching "speech delay" and getting overwhelmed by worst-case information.

Nurélie keeps this practical and non-diagnostic: everyday ways to support language, plus clear, honest guidance on when a speech-language professional is worth talking to.

How Nurélie can help

Everyday language-building ideas

Practical ways to encourage talking during meals, play, and routines, suited to your child's age.

Non-diagnostic pattern awareness

Nurélie can help you understand common patterns in speech and language development, without ever telling you your child has (or doesn't have) a delay.

When to seek support

Honest guidance on when a pediatrician or speech-language pathologist evaluation is worth pursuing.

IEP & Early Intervention support

If you're already working with a specialist, Nurélie's IEP tools can help you stay organized.

Built around your child

Nurélie factors in your child's age, home language environment, and what you've observed, so its guidance reflects your specific child rather than a generic milestone chart.

Generic advice vs. personalized guidance

Generic search

"2 year old not talking" returns a mix of milestone charts and alarming forum posts, with no sense of your specific child's situation.

With Nurélie

You tell Harlow your child understands everything you say, points and gestures clearly, but says fewer than 20 words at 2, and your household speaks two languages at home. Nurélie helps you separate what's common in multilingual development from signals worth raising with a pediatrician.

A partner, not a clinician.

Nurélie is not a speech-language pathologist and does not diagnose speech or language delays. If you have any concern about your child's speech, language, or hearing, please talk with your pediatrician, who can refer you to a speech-language pathologist for a full evaluation.

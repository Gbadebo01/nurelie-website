Harlow

Tell Harlow what happened.

Harlow is Nurélie's AI parenting companion. Talk or type, the way you'd tell a friend. Harlow uses your child's age, temperament, routines, and history to help you think through what to try next — not a script, a conversation.

Guided Tasks

Advice becomes action.

A Guided Task turns a conversation with Harlow into something concrete: a practical step to try, why it may help, and what to notice afterward — so you're not left holding advice with nowhere to put it.

Family Command

Parenting doesn't happen alone.

Parent, partner, co-parent, grandparent, babysitter, nanny — every trusted caregiver can share one family context: shared tasks, shared schedules, and updates, with role-aware access.

Learn about Family Command →

A family is not eight separate problems.

Sleep can influence behavior. Behavior can influence parent stress. Parent stress can influence routines. Speech and development support may involve outside specialists. Strategies only work consistently when every caregiver understands them. Nurélie treats your family as one connected system — not eight separate problems to solve in isolation.

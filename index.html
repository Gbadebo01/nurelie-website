Overwhelmed

Parenting information isn't scarce. It's scattered.

Right now, a typical parent is piecing together guidance from Google searches and parenting blogs, Reddit threads and social media, generic AI tools, pediatrician appointments and therapists, child development milestone checklists, sleep apps and bedtime routines, calendars, notes, and texts between caregivers, school and IEP documents — and whatever finally worked during last night's toddler tantrums, half-remembered by morning.

The problem was never a lack of information. It's knowing what applies to your child, your family, this particular moment. Nurélie exists to close that gap — replacing second-guessing with informed, personalized, practical guidance.

Curious

Everything a family needs, connected.

Eight areas. One companion holding them together — so support for one part of family life informs every other part.

Guidance

Personalized Parenting Guidance

Tell Harlow what's happening — tantrums, discipline, emotional regulation, difficult transitions, morning routines, bedtime struggles, sibling conflict, school challenges — and get gentle, positive parenting strategies shaped by your child's profile, not a reading list.

Behavior

Behavior & Routine Support

Why does your toddler have meltdowns, and why do they always seem to hit at the same time of day? Understand the pattern behind the difficult moment — tantrums, transitions, bedtime, mornings, public outings, and discipline — weighted by your child's developmental stage and temperament.

Speech

Speech & Development

Concerned about a speech delay, or looking for simple ways to encourage your toddler to talk? Track milestones, speech sounds, words, and language progress over time, with guided at-home activities — built to complement the work of a speech-language therapist, not replace it.

Sleep

Sleep Support

If your toddler is fighting bedtime, waking every night, or moving through a sleep regression, understand what the pattern actually means — age-appropriate expectations and what to try next — with guidance personalized to your child.

Wellness

Parent Wellness

For the overwhelmed, exhausted parent: mood, stress, and reflection check-ins alongside calm, reset moments. Parenting burnout is real, and your child is supported when you are — the parent and child are never treated as two unrelated systems.

Coordination

Family Command

The shared environment that keeps every caregiver — co-parent, grandparent, sitter, teacher — working from the same family routines, information, tasks, and schedule.

IEP

IEP & Early Intervention Support

For families navigating therapy, early intervention, an IEP, or a 504 plan: a place to organize key dates, goals, therapy notes, questions, and meeting preparation — so you can advocate confidently at school.

Specialists

Specialist Support

Technology can't replace human expertise. When a family needs more — including occupational therapists for sensory processing needs, or specialists experienced with neurodivergent children — Nurélie helps bridge the gap to qualified speech-language professionals, behavior specialists, and developmental experts.

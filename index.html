Overwhelmed

Parenting information isn't scarce. It's scattered.

Right now, a typical parent is piecing together guidance from Google searches and parenting blogs, Reddit threads and social media, generic AI tools, pediatrician appointments and therapists, developmental resources, sleep apps and milestone trackers, calendars, notes, and texts between caregivers, school and IEP documents — and their own memory of what worked last time.

The problem was never a lack of information. It's knowing what applies to your child, your family, this particular moment. Nurélie exists to close that gap — replacing second-guessing with informed, personalized, practical guidance.

Understood

Guidance

Personalized Parenting Guidance

Tell Harlow what's happening — tantrums, discipline, emotional regulation, difficult transitions, morning routines, bedtime struggles, sibling conflict, school challenges — and get practical next steps shaped by your child's profile, not a reading list.

Behavior

Behavior & Routine Support

Understand the pattern behind the difficult moment. Guidance on tantrums, transitions, bedtime, mornings, public outings, and discipline, weighted by your child's developmental stage and temperament.

Speech

Speech & Development

Track milestones, speech sounds, words, and language progress over time, with guided at-home activities — built to complement the work of a speech-language therapist, not replace it.

Sleep

Sleep Support

Log sleep and bedtime routines and understand what the patterns actually mean — regressions, age-appropriate expectations, and what to try next — with guidance personalized to your child.

Wellness

Parent Wellness

Mood, stress, and reflection check-ins alongside calm, reset moments. Your child is supported when you are — the parent and child are never treated as two unrelated systems.

Coordination

Family Command

The shared environment that keeps every caregiver — co-parent, grandparent, sitter, teacher — working from the same information, tasks, and schedule.

IEP

IEP & Early Intervention Support

Specialists

Specialist Support

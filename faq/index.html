About Nurélie

Nurélie is a personalized parenting support app built around Harlow, an AI parenting companion, and Family Command, a shared space for caregivers. It helps with everyday challenges like behavior, tantrums, routines, sleep, and development.

It's a support tool that adjusts its guidance to your specific child and family — using context like age, temperament, and history — rather than giving everyone the same generic advice.

Nurélie is designed to support parents and caregivers from infancy through adolescence, with guidance that adapts as your child grows.

Harlow

Harlow is Nurélie's AI parenting companion. You tell Harlow what's going on with your child, in your own words, and it helps you think through what to try next.

Generic AI tools know parenting in general. Harlow is built to hold your family's specific context — your child's age, patterns, and history — across conversations, so guidance builds on what it already knows about your kid, not a fresh generic answer every time.

Nurélie supports natural text and voice input to Harlow, so you can describe a situation however is easiest in the moment.

Personalization

Nurélie uses what you share about your child's age, temperament, strengths, routines, behavioral patterns, sleep context, development, and what's worked before, along with your family's structure and preferences.

Nurélie builds a picture of your family over time from what you share and what you tell it has helped before, so guidance can reflect your child's actual history rather than starting over each time.

Development & Milestones

Understand What to Expect as Your Child Grows

Milestone charts tell you what's typical on average. Nurélie helps you think through what you're seeing in your specific child, and practical ways to support the next stage.

Home/Parenting Challenges/Development & Milestones

"Is my child developing normally?" is one of the most common — and most anxiety-inducing — parenting questions. Milestone charts are useful, but they flatten a wide range of typical development into a single line.

Nurélie helps you think through your child's development in context: age, what they're already doing, and what you're noticing — without diagnosing anything.

What this can look like at home

  • Your child hit some milestones early and others later, and you're not sure what that means.
  • A well-meaning relative compared your child unfavorably to another kid the same age.
  • You want practical, age-appropriate activities to support development, not just a checklist.
  • You're not sure whether something you noticed is worth mentioning at the next pediatrician visit.

Why the same behavior can have different reasons

We don't diagnose why — but the same moment often has more than one cause, and that shapes what helps:

  • Development isn't linear — kids often progress unevenly across physical, language, social, and cognitive areas.
  • Milestone ranges are wide by design, because typical development varies significantly between children.
  • Environment, practice, and opportunity all influence when a skill shows up.
  • A single delayed milestone rarely means anything on its own — the full picture matters more.

What parents often try

  • Comparing against a printed or app-based milestone checklist.
  • Searching a specific milestone plus "delayed" and reading worst-case scenarios.
  • Asking other parents in a group chat what's "normal."

Nurélie helps you look at the whole picture of your child's development, not a single checkbox, and suggests practical next steps rather than just more information to worry over.

How Nurélie can help

Whole-picture context

Harlow considers your child's age and multiple areas of development together, not one isolated milestone.

Practical activities

Age-appropriate ideas to support the next stage of development through everyday play.

Non-diagnostic guidance

Nurélie helps you understand general patterns and prepare questions for your pediatrician — it doesn't diagnose developmental conditions.

A running family profile

Keep a record of what you're noticing over time, useful context to bring to appointments.

Built around your child

Nurélie factors in your child's age and what you've told it they're already doing, so guidance reflects your specific child's developmental picture rather than a single generic chart.

Generic advice vs. personalized guidance

Generic search

"3 year old development" returns a static milestone checklist with no context for your specific child's strengths or your actual concerns.

With Nurélie

You tell Harlow your 3-year-old is verbally advanced but resists using utensils and prefers hands, and you're unsure if that's a preference or worth mentioning. Nurélie helps you think through the context and prepare a clear, specific question for your pediatrician.

A partner, not a clinician.

Nurélie does not diagnose developmental delays or conditions. If you have concerns about your child's development in any area, your pediatrician is the right first step, and can refer you to a specialist for a full evaluation if needed.

Frequently asked questions

Development varies widely between children. Nurélie can help you think through what you're noticing, but a pediatrician is best positioned to assess your child directly.

Milestones are general markers — physical, language, social, cognitive — that describe typical ranges for when skills tend to emerge. They're guides, not guarantees.

Nurélie can help you keep a running picture of what you've noticed and discussed, which can be useful context for pediatrician visits.

Any time you're concerned is a reasonable time to talk to your pediatrician. Nurélie can help you organize your thoughts before that conversation.
